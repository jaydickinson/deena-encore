Perspective / Draft article

Illustrative editorial content for this page design. Final story to be supplied.

An event is more than the time spent in front of a stage. It is a journey made up of invitations, arrivals, conversations, content and the moments that follow.

Walk through the whole journey.

Looking at the experience from an attendee’s perspective can reveal questions that a floor plan alone cannot answer. Where will people go first? What do they need to find? How will they know what is happening next?

Mapping that journey helps connect the physical environment with the information and support people need.

A useful questionWhat does someone need at this moment to feel welcome, informed and involved?

Consider different ways to take part.

People arrive with different needs and preferences. Clear wayfinding, accessible routes, readable content and places to step away can all be considered as part of the design process.

  • Make essential information easy to find before arrival.
  • Review the route between key spaces.
  • Balance focused sessions with opportunities to connect.

Give each moment a purpose.

An immersive experience does not need every moment to be busy. A thoughtful rhythm can give audiences time to listen, reflect, explore and contribute.

Starting with the audience keeps the design focused on what the experience should help them feel, understand and remember.
