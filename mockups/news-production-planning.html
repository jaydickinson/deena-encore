The planning behind a seamless show

A clear technical plan connects the creative ambition with everything needed to deliver it in the room.

When a show feels effortless, there is usually a detailed plan behind it. Technical production turns an idea into a sequence of practical decisions, shared responsibilities and carefully prepared moments.

Bring the teams together early.

Lighting, sound, video, staging and rigging affect one another. Reviewing them together helps establish what the space can support and where the creative ambition needs a more detailed solution.

A useful starting point is a shared view of the event format, audience, venue, content requirements and available rehearsal time.

Turn the plan into a running order.

A running order connects the audience experience to the work happening behind the scenes. It should make it clear what happens, who is responsible and what each department needs to prepare.

  • Map arrivals, presentations, transitions and breaks.
  • Confirm content formats and delivery deadlines.
  • Allow time to test key moments with the people delivering them.
A practical principleClarity behind the scenes gives the live experience room to breathe.

Rehearse the transitions.

The spaces between headline moments deserve attention too. Speaker changes, walk-on music, video playback and lighting cues all shape the pace of a show.

Rehearsal creates a chance to check those connections, agree communication and review alternative approaches before the audience arrives.
